Questions? Answers.

Is Pulsar really free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads. It connects you to free networks run by Cloudflare (WARP), the non-profit Riseup, and the VPN Gate academic project.

Does Pulsar collect my data?

The app itself sends nothing to us. Your traffic goes through the network you connect to — Cloudflare, Riseup or VPN Gate — under their own policies.

Will it make my internet faster?

A VPN can’t be faster than your own internet plan. Pulsar picks the closest, quickest server so you lose as little as possible — usually almost nothing.

Why does it ask for administrator rights?

Creating a VPN tunnel changes how Windows routes network traffic, which only an administrator may do. On first use Pulsar may install the free Cloudflare WARP or OpenVPN engine.

Why does Windows warn me?

Windows shows that message for apps that aren’t signed with a paid certificate. Pulsar is independently made; click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
